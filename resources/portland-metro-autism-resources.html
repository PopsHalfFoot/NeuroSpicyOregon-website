Before you make a referral

  • Are new participants being accepted? What is the actual wait?
  • Is a formal diagnosis or county I/DD eligibility needed?
  • What does it cost, and which funding or insurance is accepted?
  • Is support provided, or should the person bring a support worker?
  • What communication, sensory, and physical access is available?
  • Is transportation included? How early must someone register?

Listings are educational starting points, not guarantees of eligibility, availability, or fit. Confirm details with each organization.
