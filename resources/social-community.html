Connect with peers

Peer & creative groups

Casual conversation, shared interests, and places to meet others.

Portland metro · Online

Portland Circle of Rainbow Brains

A peer social group for neurodivergent adults 18+. It welcomes self-identified members and is LGBTQIA2S+ friendly. Activities include local gatherings and virtual craft/chat meetups; some plans are shared in the group's Slack space.

  • Adults 18+
  • Peer group
  • In person & online

Dues are optional. Message the organizer through Meetup to ask about access and current events.

Visit organizer's page ↗

Portland metro · Online

Autism Empowerment: Neurodivergent Creators Collective

A neurodivergent-led creative community for adults and teens interested in writing, media, performance, and other creative work. It offers workshops and a monthly online Chill & Chat.

  • Teens & adults
  • Creative community
  • In person & online

Membership is free. See the group's event page for upcoming sessions.

Visit organizer's page ↗

Bend

The Base at Franklin

A community space where neurodivergent members can connect in person and join peer-offered groups. A private social platform shares activities and resources.

  • Community space
  • Member-led
  • In person

The organization describes the program as free. Contact The Base for membership access and the current schedule.

Visit organizer's page ↗

Eugene · Lane County

KindTree – Autism Rocks

KindTree lists an Eugene peer support group for autistic adults and a separate social-skills discussion series. Participants choose community outings in alternate months.

  • Adults
  • Peer support
  • In person

Check the event list for each group's next date, location, and participation details.

Visit organizer's events ↗

Statewide · Online

Autism Society of Oregon online groups

ASO hosts distinct social and support groups, including a young-adult social group for ages 18–30, an intersectional-identity group for autistic adults, a supported teen and young-adult club, and adult discussion groups.

  • Age-specific groups
  • Peer support
  • Online

Eligibility and registration vary by group. Choose a group on ASO's page for current details.

Visit organizer's groups ↗

Get out together

Outings & recreation

Community activities for different ages and support needs.

Portland metro · Summer

Autistic Community Activity Program (ACAP)

ACAP's summer day camp combines a familiar routine with community outings and public-transit practice for autistic participants.

  • Ages 7+
  • Summer day camp
  • In person

ACAP says a primary ASD diagnosis is required. Check the camp page for the next season's application, fees, and openings.

Visit organizer's camp page ↗

Central Oregon

Central Oregon Autism Movement (COAM)

COAM hosts family and community activities, including sensory-friendly events, swims, and seasonal gatherings in Central Oregon.

  • Families
  • Sensory-friendly events
  • In person

COAM describes its programs as free. Some events require advance RSVP and can fill; check each event's audience and location.

Visit organizer's events ↗

Central & Eastern Oregon

Central Oregon Disability Support Network (CODSN)

CODSN offers separate youth meetups, youth and young-adult activities, and parent or caregiver gatherings in several communities.

  • Youth & caregivers
  • Community meetups
  • In person

Each gathering has its own age range, location, and RSVP details. Review the specific meetup before attending.

Visit organizer's meetups ↗

Bend

Bend Park & Recreation: Therapeutic Recreation

Inclusive programs for people of all abilities include adult outings, dancing, social clubs, arts, and physical activities, plus youth and teen options.

  • Youth & adults
  • Inclusive recreation
  • In person

Offerings, age ranges, fees, and registration vary. Use the current district activity guide.

Visit district program page ↗

Structured support

Guided social groups

A therapy-based option for teens who want to practice friendship and communication skills.

Portland · South Waterfront

OneYou Psychology: Teen PEERS group

OneYou advertises an in-person, therapist-led PEERS group for teens focused on friendship and communication. Its separate Teen ADHD Group focuses mainly on executive-function skills.

  • Teens
  • Therapist-led
  • In person

Contact the practice for age fit, next group dates, intake, insurance, fees, and openings.
