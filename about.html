ABOUT NEURO SPICY OREGON

Different minds belong here.

Neuro Spicy Oregon is building a welcoming community where neurodivergent people, families, allies, and organizations can connect, learn, share resources, and celebrate the strengths of different kinds of minds.

Why Neuro Spicy?

Different doesn't mean less.

Neurodiversity recognizes that human brains naturally work in different ways. Autism, ADHD, dyslexia, and other forms of neurodivergence are part of the diversity of the human experience.

Neuro Spicy Oregon exists to create connection, increase understanding, share useful resources, and build a community where people don't have to hide the way their minds work.

We believe different minds don't just belong in our communities — they make our communities stronger.

What We Stand For

Our mission is bigger than awareness.

We're building a community centered on belonging, understanding, connection, and celebrating the strengths of neurodivergent minds.

01

Purpose

To create a world where different minds, different abilities, and different ways of living are recognized as strengths—and where people have the opportunity, community, and resources to build meaningful lives on their own terms.

02

Vision

A world where different minds aren’t merely accommodated—they’re valued, empowered, and given room to thrive.

03

Values

Keep It Neuro Spicy! This is the value that ties everything together: embrace the quirks, challenge the ordinary, care deeply, create boldly, and never make “normal” the price of belonging.

04

Celebration

Move beyond simply accepting differences and celebrate the creativity, perspectives, talents, and strengths different minds bring to the world.
