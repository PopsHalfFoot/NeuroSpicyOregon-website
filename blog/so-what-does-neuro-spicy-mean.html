Neuro Spicy 101

So… What Does
“Neuro Spicy” Mean?

A fun, informal way of talking about brains that don't always work the “standard” way.

August 2026

The short answer is: it’s a fun, informal way of talking about brains that don’t always work the “standard” way.

The longer answer is a little more interesting.

People may use it to describe themselves when they experience the world in ways that differ from what is often considered “typical.” That might include people who identify as neurodivergent, including people with ADHD, autism, dyslexia, Tourette syndrome, and other neurological differences.

For some people, the phrase is simply a humorous way of acknowledging that their brain has its own operating system.

And sometimes that operating system comes with a few unexpected features.

Where did I put my phone?

I don't know.

I'm holding it.

01

Just Like There Is Biodiversity… There Is Neurodiversity

Human brains are wonderfully weird.

Some people can walk into a crowded room and barely notice the noise. Someone else might immediately notice every conversation, every flickering light, the music in the background, and the person chewing gum three tables away.

Some people can sit down and calmly work through a ten-step process.

Someone else might look at those ten steps and think, “Sure, but what if we did steps 7, 2, 9, and then accidentally invented a completely different system?”

Some people love routines.

Some people need routines.

Some people make routines and then immediately rebel against their own routines.

Brains are different.

And that's not necessarily a bad thing.

02

Different. Not Less.

One of the ideas we want to push back against is the assumption that there is only one “right” way for a brain to work.

Different brains have different strengths, challenges, preferences, sensitivities, and ways of making sense of the world.

Sometimes those differences can make life harder.

Sometimes they can make life fascinating.

Sometimes they do both before breakfast.

DIFFERENT. NOT LESS.

Being different doesn't mean there is something wrong with the way your brain works.

Being neurodivergent doesn't mean someone is automatically brilliant, creative, disabled, difficult, gifted, or any other single thing.

People are more complicated than that.

That's part of the point.

03

Different Minds, Different Ways of Being

We aren't interested in putting people into neat little boxes.

We're interested in creating a place where people can say:

“This is how my brain works.”

And someone else can respond:

“Cool. Mine works differently.”

And that's okay.

Maybe there are just a whole lot of different ways to be human.

04

Making Room for Different Minds

Because the goal isn't to make everyone think the same way.

It's not to make everyone behave the same way.

It's not even to make everyone agree.

It's to create space for different minds to exist, connect, learn, laugh, and be themselves.

THE IDEA

Life would be pretty boring if every brain came with the same settings.

A Little Spicy

Bring Your Weird. Bring Your Wonderful.

Bring your questions.

There's room here for different minds, different experiences, and different ways of seeing the world.

Because being different doesn't mean being less.

It means you're human.

Keep it spicy!
