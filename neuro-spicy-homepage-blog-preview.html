Why Neuro Spicy?

A different way of seeing the world.

Neuro Spicy is a playful, informal term some people use to describe themselves as neurodivergent.

It celebrates the idea that different brains bring different ways of thinking, learning, communicating, creating, and experiencing the world.

Someone who identifies as neurospicy may be autistic, have ADHD, dyslexia, dyspraxia, Tourette syndrome, or another neurological difference.

Not everyone who is neurodivergent uses the term neurospicy, and that's okay. It's a personal choice.

At Neuro Spicy, we use the term with warmth, humor, and pride.

Understanding Neurodiversity

Different brains are part of being human.

We want to help create a community where different ways of thinking, learning, communicating, and experiencing the world are understood, respected, and supported.

What Is Neurodiversity?

Many ways to experience the world.

Neurodiversity is the idea that differences in how human brains develop and function are a natural part of human diversity.

People can differ in how they process information, communicate, learn, focus, socialize, regulate emotions, respond to sensory experiences, and interact with the world.

Our Perspective

Support the person. Celebrate the person.

Neurodiversity does not mean ignoring disability, challenges, or the need for support.

It means providing appropriate accommodations and services while recognizing people's strengths, individuality, dignity, and potential.

Who We Are

This is the beginning.

Brian, Wendy, Adrienne, and like-minded friends got together and started talking about what it would look like to build something of our own.

We imagined a homestead where people could come together, grow things, care for the land, build businesses, spend time outdoors, and create a community where people understand and celebrate neurodiversity.

We know that vision won't happen overnight.

That's why we're documenting the journey from the very beginning — including the ideas, research, setbacks, discoveries, land search, development plans, and everything we learn along the way.

We're starting with an idea. Now we're building the path.

The Search

First, we need somewhere to grow.

One of the biggest steps in our journey will be finding the right piece of Oregon land for our long-term vision.

We're looking for property that could eventually support some combination of farming, forestry, animals, recreation, events, community, and future development.

We don't have all the answers yet. That's part of the journey.

STEP 01

Define the Vision

Research what we need from land, infrastructure, zoning, water, access, and surrounding community.

STEP 02

Find the Land

Explore Oregon properties and identify land that could support our long-term goals.

STEP 03

Build Thoughtfully

Develop the property gradually, responsibly, and with the land and community in mind.

STEP 04

Grow

Establish gardens, specialty crops, animals, and other agricultural projects.

STEP 05

Create

Explore products, events, experiences, and businesses that grow naturally from the land.

STEP 06

Share

Create opportunities for people to learn, connect, participate, and belong.
