Welcome

We're just getting started.

Neuro Spicy Collective is an Oregon-based collective founded around a simple idea: people with different ways of thinking, learning, creating, and experiencing the world deserve places where they can belong, contribute, and thrive.

We're at the beginning of our journey. Our long-term vision includes finding land where we can develop a regenerative homestead, grow specialty herbs and other crops, practice responsible land stewardship, host camping and community events, and create opportunities for neurodiversity awareness and education.

We don't have the finished property yet. We don't have all the answers yet. We're building the vision one step at a time.
Why Neuro Spicy?

Different brains bring different possibilities.

Neurospicy is a playful, informal term some people use to describe themselves as neurodivergent.

It celebrates the idea that different brains bring different ways of thinking, learning, communicating, creating, and experiencing the world.

Someone who identifies as neurospicy may be autistic, have ADHD, dyslexia, dyspraxia, Tourette syndrome, or another neurological difference.

Not everyone who is neurodivergent uses the term neurospicy, and that's okay. It's a personal choice.

What Is Neurodiversity?

Neurodiversity is the idea that differences in how human brains develop and function are a natural part of human diversity.


People can differ in how they process information, communicate, learn, focus, socialize, regulate emotions, respond to sensory experiences, and interact with the world around them.


The neurodiversity perspective recognizes these differences without assuming there is only one "normal" or correct way for a brain to function.

Keep It Neuro Spicy.

Our goal is simple: build a community where different minds are understood, supported, included, and celebrated.

Who Are We?

A few people with a big idea.

Brian, Wendy, Adrienne, and a few like-minded friends started talking about how great it would be to create a homestead and eventually develop businesses and a community around it.

A place where people could live, work, create, learn, and build community with people who understand, celebrate, and support neurodiversity.

That conversation became Neuro Spicy Collective.

"What if we could build a place where different minds could grow together?"
What We're Interested In

Many interests. One bigger vision.

These are the areas we're exploring as we develop Neuro Spicy Collective and work toward finding the right land for our future.

🌱

Regenerative Farming

Building healthy soil and growing specialty herbs and future crops using regenerative and no-till practices.

🌲

Land & Forestry

Learning how to responsibly steward forests, fields, habitat, water, and the natural systems around us.

🏡

Homesteading

Developing practical skills around growing food, caring for animals, maintaining land, and creating a resilient home base.

🐕

Mastadors

One of our future interests is responsible Mastador breeding, with an emphasis on healthy, well-socialized dogs.

⛺

Camping & Events

We envision eventually creating a place where people can gather for camping, events, workshops, education, and community.

🧠

Neurodiversity

Increasing awareness, understanding, acceptance, and inclusion of neurodivergent people and their experiences.
