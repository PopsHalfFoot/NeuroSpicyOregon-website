Nature Neuroscience · 2026

Large International Study Identifies 36 Risk Genes Across OCD and Chronic Tic Disorders

Whole-exome sequencing from thousands of people with obsessive–compulsive disorder, chronic tic disorders, or both expands the list of high-confidence large-effect risk genes and strengthens evidence that the two conditions share part of their genetic architecture.

What it does not mean: researchers did not discover “the Tourette gene,” a new diagnostic test, or a new treatment.

How Autism-Linked Mutations Rewire Protein Networks

Researchers mapped 1,881 protein interactions across 100 high-confidence autism risk proteins and found that different patient-derived mutations can converge on shared molecular systems.

What it does not mean: this is not “the cause of autism,” a diagnostic test, or a treatment announcement.

Exciting findings need context.

01Start with the original study whenever possible.

04Link the source so readers can check us.

LAB NOTE

Research summaries are education, not medical advice.

A single study rarely settles a scientific question. Findings can change with larger samples, different populations, new methods, or replication. These explainers are designed to help readers understand research, not diagnose conditions or make treatment decisions.
